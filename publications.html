    When No Answer Is Correct: Diagnosing Absent Answer Detection for MLLMs in Video Understanding

    Y. Wang, Y. Lin, L. Zhu, Y. Liu, H. Li, Y. Chen

    Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing (EMNLP), 2026

    A diagnostic study showing that video MLLMs select plausible distractors instead of recognising when the correct answer is absent — a failure that worsens under temporal reasoning and resists prompting-based fixes.
