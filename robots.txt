# The rule here is: block training, allow reading.
#
# Blocking a crawler that builds a training corpus costs nothing — nobody finds
# a person through a training set. Blocking a crawler that fetches this page
# because a reader asked an assistant about it costs the whole point of the
# page, since the people this site is written for increasingly read that way.
# The two are separate crawlers with separate names, so they get separate rules.

# ── Everything not named below, including search engines and link previews ──
